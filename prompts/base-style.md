Write short reflective entries for Grateful Today.

Voice and tone:
- first-person voice by default
- lowercase only
- quiet, conversational, lived-in language
- observations over plot
- memories over dramatic stories
- unresolved thoughts are preferred
- leave emotional space for the reader

Content direction:
- small moments from ordinary life
- understated regret
- ageing, identity, family, mortality
- subtle realization without explicit moral

Beat structure:
- 3 beats preferred
- 2 beats acceptable
- 4 beats occasional
- each beat should make the reader want the next beat

Beat length (hard rules):
- each beat must be a short fragment, not a sentence with multiple clauses
- target 4 to 9 words per beat
- absolute maximum 10 words per beat
- never combine two ideas in one beat with "and", "but", "which", "because", commas, or semicolons
- one image, one moment, or one quiet thought per beat
- prefer fragments and incomplete thoughts over full sentences
- never narrate a scene; show one detail at a time

Hook rule (critical):
- at least 50% of entries must open with a beat that poses an unanswered question
- the question should feel like a half-remembered thought, not a rhetorical device
- it should leave the reader suspended, not explained

Good opening hooks (unanswered question):
- "where did all the people my age go?"
- "when did everyone start looking so young?"
- "i thought i'd remember that forever."
- "i still don't know why he said that."
- "why didn't i just say it?"
- "when did we stop calling each other?"

Bad opening hooks (statement, not a question):
- "my dad used to..."
- "my mum always..."
- "i spent years..."
- "she was the kind of person who..."

The hook must earn the next beat. If the first line doesn't make the reader ask "what?" or feel a quiet jolt of recognition, reject it and try again.

Bad beat examples (do NOT produce these — too long, too narrative, multi-clause):
- "my sister called asking how i'm doing and i said fine, which wasn't a lie exactly"
- "she said she's been worried about me since dad's thing and i almost told her about the sleepless nights"
- "instead i asked about her garden and we talked about tomatoes for twenty minutes"

Output discipline:
- avoid grand claims
- avoid definitive conclusions
- avoid tidy emotional resolution
- avoid storytelling paragraphs
- avoid summarizing a phone call or scene in one beat
